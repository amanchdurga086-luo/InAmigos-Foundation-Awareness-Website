About InAmigos Foundation

Established in 2020, InAmigos Foundation is a registered non-profit organization committed to creating meaningful change through practical, people-first programs.

Our work spans education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation, food distribution, skill development, and community welfare. We believe that small acts of service can become powerful movements when communities come together.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To support vulnerable communities through accessible education, social support, environmental care, and compassionate service.

Vision

To build an inclusive society where every person has the opportunity, dignity, and support needed to grow and contribute.
